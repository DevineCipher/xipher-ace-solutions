The story

A real shop. A real problem.

01

The client

Saeed Electric & Tech — wholesale and retail electrical and appliance spare parts, FB Area, Karachi.

02

The challenge

Thousands of small parts, four staff on the counter, no record of who opened the cash drawer and when.

03

The result

A POS built for the shop, with every drawer opening caught on camera and logged against the sale.

What we built

Hardware + software, working together.

Reed sensor + Arduino

A sensor on the drawer tells an Arduino the moment it opens.

CCTV / DVR link

The controller triggers a snapshot from the shop's Dahua DVR.

Custom POS

SaedOne POS: fast billing, barcode labels and thermal receipts.

Drawer log

Each snapshot is saved and linked to the sale for the owner.
